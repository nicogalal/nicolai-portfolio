About

Bio

Nicolai is a global creative executive who treats creative as a product discipline: built, measured, and iterated against real commercial outcomes rather than shipped and forgotten. As VP, Global Brand & Creative at Podimo, he built a cross-market creative platform and launch framework that lifted conversion metrics up to 50% while scaling creative output 650% through AI-driven production.

Former Walt Disney Company creative leader, where he directed the creative vision for the $2B Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge launch and personally authored its global style guide. Across entertainment, fintech, and consumer brands, he builds small, autonomous teams that ship fast and own outcomes.

He leads through people and systems in equal measure. The people part: hiring strong, complementary talent, trusting their skills, and leading with high EQ and a bias for finding the solution rather than the problem. The systems part: giving creativity an engine, like the test-and-learn loop he built between creative and growth, where proven formats are iterated on signal (hook, hold, conversion), two to three new creative bets are tested head-to-head each month, and every asset ends in one of four decisions: optimise, scale, re-test, or kill. Each cycle closes on five questions: what did we test, what did we expect, what did the data show, what did we learn creatively, and what's next.
